Microsoft WPF Fluent theme
--------------------------

The styles of PowerPane's Settings window are adapted from Microsoft's Fluent theme
for WPF, which ships as PresentationFramework.Fluent with .NET 9 WPF.

  Source repository: https://github.com/dotnet/wpf
  Source commit:     dbd3026982f09a279e4595666c07bb6136f579c3
  Source path:       src/Microsoft.DotNet.Wpf/src/Themes/PresentationFramework.Fluent/
  In the source:     PowerPane/Themes/Fluent/ (see NOTICE.md there for what was
                     ported and what PowerPane changed)
